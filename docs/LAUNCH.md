# Human Machine launch film

**57 seconds.** An animated walkthrough of picking people, applying their public ideas, comparing perspectives, mixing them with your experience and keeping a useful lesson. Original music and pixel-art portraits.

[Watch the film](https://ludogranger.github.io/human-machine/) using the small play button beside the Human Machine wordmark.

## Trump: a historical replay

The film uses Trump's **April 9, 2025 public tariff-pause announcement**. It is a public statement, not access to private thinking. Country-specific reciprocal tariffs were paused for non-retaliating countries; the 10% baseline remained and China was excluded.

- [Presidential statement archived by UCSB](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/statement-united-states-tariff-adjustments)
- [Official order](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202500459/pdf/DCPD-202500459.pdf)
- [Original Truth Social post](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/114309144289505174)

The hypothetical replay buys SPY at the **April 9 closing price of $548.62**, after the announcement, and exits at the **May 9 closing price of $564.34**. It does not capture the earlier same-day rally.

`$1,000 × $564.34 / $548.62 = $1,028.65`

The result is **+$28.65 (+2.87%)**, with a **−6.33% worst closing drawdown**. The next day was negative; the portfolio fell to a closing value of $936.68 on April 21 before recovering. The chart uses 22 daily closing observations.

The event and holding period were selected **in hindsight**. Fractional shares and frictionless closing-price execution are assumed. Fees, taxes, slippage and dividends are excluded. No trades were executed and this is not a customer result. Same-window SPY buy-and-hold has the identical result: **no outperformance or repeatable trading edge is demonstrated**. Historical performance does not establish future performance.

Price sources: [ChartExchange daily history](https://chartexchange.com/symbol/nyse-spy/historical/), [FinanceCharts SPY prices](https://www.financecharts.com/etfs/SPY/summary/price) and a [Citi pricing-supplement reproduction](https://www.streetinsider.com/SEC%2BFilings/Form%2B424B2%2BCITIGROUP%2BINC/24625722.html) cross-checking the April 9 close. This data was researched specifically for the demonstration; it is not a claim that Human Machine's production crawler captured or traded the event.

## Garry: a worked founder example

The visible quote comes from [Garry Tan's September 24, 2026 public X post](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2102955139875397806). His [August 25 post about making artifacts and experiments](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2092059517446156640) also informs the worked example. Both posts were manually selected and verified through X's public embed endpoint. The film does not demonstrate automated live X ingestion.

**Slot** is a fictional agent-friendly booking prototype for repair shops, created for this film. The five customer interviews are planned, not completed. No customer traction, revenue, real bookings or actual company launch are claimed. Compare, Mix and Keep are illustrated workflow steps; the principle shown was saved as a demonstration project note, not as a write to a user's personal GBrain. The film redraws this worked prototype in a motion layout; it is not a recording of an autonomous end-to-end backend run.
