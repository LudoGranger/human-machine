## Trump / Tesla: a historical replay

The event is the public result of the 2024 US presidential election. AP records its Trump victory call at **05:34 Eastern on November 6, 2024**. In public victory remarks that day, Trump also praised Elon Musk. The example interprets public information; it does not expose private thinking or establish that the election caused the entire subsequent stock move.

- [AP's own race-call record](https://www.ap.org/the-definitive-source/behind-the-news/calling-the-2024-presidential-race-state-by-state/)
- [Archived Trump victory remarks](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/remarks-accepting-election-the-47th-president-the-united-states-palm-beach-florida)

The hypothetical position buys TSLA at the **November 6 closing price of $288.53**, after the public result, and exits at the **December 6 closing price of $389.22**. It excludes the election-night and November 6 intraday jump.

`$1,000 × $389.22 / $288.53 = $1,348.98`

The gain is **$348.98 (+34.90%)** over 30 calendar days. The **worst closing drawdown was −11.09%**, from the November 11 close of $350.00 to the November 14 close of $311.18. This is a decline from a prior peak, not an 11.09% loss of starting capital. The chart uses 22 daily closing observations.

Same-window SPY went from $591.04 to $607.81, returning **+2.84%**. TSLA's return exceeded SPY by 32.06 percentage points in this selected window. This is not risk-adjusted alpha: a concentrated single stock has different risk from a broad index.

**The stock, event and holding period were selected in hindsight.** This is a winning illustrative scenario, not a pre-registered strategy, causal study, forecast, actual trade or customer result. It assumes fractional shares, price-only returns and frictionless closing-price execution. Fees, taxes, slippage and dividend reinvestment are excluded. No repeatable investing edge is established; historical performance does not establish future performance.

## Garry / a worked founder example

The short quote comes from [Garry Tan's September 24, 2026 public X post](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2102955139875397806). His [August 25 post about artifacts and experiments](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2092059517446156640) also informs the example. Both posts were manually selected and verified through X's public embed endpoint. This is not a demonstration of automatic live X ingestion.

**Slot** is a fictional agent-friendly booking prototype for repair shops, created for the film. Five interviews are planned, not completed. No customer traction, revenue, actual bookings or company launch are claimed. The film redraws the prototype in a motion layout; it is not a recording of an autonomous end-to-end backend run. The Keep principle was saved as a demo project note, not written to a user's personal GBrain.
