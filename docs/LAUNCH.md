# Human Machine — your evolving brain

**An evolving version of you, shaped by the humans you pick.**

This 56-second film shows a continuous learning loop: Pick → Catch up → Ask → Compare → Mix → Keep. Fresh public ideas inform your work; your judgment and feedback become lessons for the next task. The product represents source-grounded perspectives, not private access to a person's thoughts or actual participation by the people pictured.

## What the film demonstrates

1. Pick Brian Chesky, Garry Tan and Andrej Karpathy as sources of inspiration.
2. Bring a dated public idea into your working context.
3. Apply it to Slot, a fictional booking prototype.
4. Add your own constraint: owners must approve requests.
5. Keep that lesson as your rule.
6. Carry it into the next task: follow-ups require owner approval.

The motion sequence is an animated worked example, not a screen recording of a live autonomous backend. The accompanying interactive prototype implements Apply, Compare, Keep, next-task inheritance and Reset. Its memory lasts in the current tab; it does not write to personal GBrain. The example constraint is hypothetical user feedback, not a statement attributed to Garry. No completed interviews, bookings, revenue or customer results are claimed.
