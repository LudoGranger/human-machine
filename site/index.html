Human Machine.

Human Machine turns the ideas of people you pick into better work with your AI.

Explore what they publish, try and change their minds about. Get feedback on your work, compare approaches, mix useful ideas with your experience, and keep what you learn.

  • What changed?
  • Why does it matter to me?
  • What can I try?
  • Did it improve my work?

You decide what to accept, adapt or reject. New evidence, your corrections and your results help your practice evolve. Your lessons remain yours; they don’t rewrite someone else’s views.

Pick anyone. Use public sources with dates, or material you supply. Perspectives are interpretations of that evidence, never private thoughts or endorsements.

Start with Copy: copy the instructions into your AI. In chat, the workflow stays in that conversation. Connect the local application for ongoing research and private GBrain memory; without connected memory, Keep gives you a note to save.
