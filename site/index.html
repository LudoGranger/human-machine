How it works

Human Machine follows what a person publishes, experiments with, changes their mind about and puts into practice. It resolves who they are, collects their public sources with provenance and dates, detects meaningful changes, and turns relevant ones into bounded exercises and agent-skill updates you can test. Everything runs on your own computer; memory lives in GBrain.

“Thinking” means an evidence-backed interpretation of public statements and actions — never private thoughts, and never an endorsement by the person.
